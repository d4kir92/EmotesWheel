# EmotesWheel

EmotesWheel opens a radial emote wheel with a keybind, similar to the ping wheel: hold the key, point the mouse at an emote and release the key to perform it.

## Features

- Opens the wheel at the mouse cursor or in the screen center.
- Two modes:
  - **Hold**: hold the key, release it to perform the selected emote.
  - **Toggle**: press to open, press again to perform the selected emote.
- Releasing in the center of the wheel cancels.
- 4 to 12 slots, each with its own emote from a list of common emotes.
- Adjustable scale.
- Three designs to choose from: Forever (default), Material Dark and Material Light.
- Emote names follow the client language.
- Settings window with search and a minimap button.

## Usage

- Open the settings with `/emoteswheel` (or `/ewheel`) or a left click on the minimap button.
- Set the key under **General → Open wheel**. Keyboard keys, modifier combinations and mouse buttons 3 to 5 work. Backspace or Delete clears the key.
- Shift + right click on the minimap button hides it.

## Notes

- Emotes run through a secure macro button, so the wheel works without restricted emote APIs.
- In combat the addon cannot change that button. On Classic clients the emote is then sent directly. On Retail a message says that wheel emotes are not possible in combat.
- Supported clients: Retail, WoW Forever, Classic Era, The Burning Crusade, Wrath, Cataclysm, and Mists of Pandaria Classic.

## Localization

Locale files exist for all World of Warcraft client locales:

`deDE`, `enUS`, `esES`, `esMX`, `frFR`, `itIT`, `koKR`, `ptBR`, `ruRU`, `zhCN`, and `zhTW`.

Translations other than English and German are machine-generated and may benefit from native-speaker review.
